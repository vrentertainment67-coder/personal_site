bio: "Cronic started DJing in 2021 for the simplest reason — he wanted to look cool — and chose formal DJ school over teaching himself off YouTube. His first gig, at a spot called Throwback near Ashok Nagar, was meant to be small; 800 people turned up, and the ₹500 he was promised for it never actually reached him. He began on psytrance, realised Bangalore wasn't the room for it, and moved through commercial pop into Bollywood. The doors opened through friends: Saurav Chauhan, who handed him that first gig; Aseem of United Sensations; Prithvi Raj and Rishi Thomas of Pulse Networks; and a manager, Dhanush, who landed him the Under 25 Summit.\n\nThen came the night that changed the trajectory — a last-minute call for the Yo Yo Honey Singh Millionaire tour, where a one-hour opening slot became three hours holding 15,000 people through pouring rain until the headliner finally walked on. After that, the bookings kept coming: Casa Bacardí on tour, a Talviinder live show, brand collaborations with Untamed and Westside, a tech rider the clubs pushed back on, and a fee that has climbed roughly five times over with ten in sight. He also built his own IP — Run & Rave, an 8am run-and-rave he started with Pulse Networks and 56 Run Club that drew 1,500 to the Mall of Asia. All of it in four years. The episode is built as a verdict: VIC lays out the evidence across talent, luck and system, Cronic defends it and scores himself out of 100 — and the sealed envelope VIC filled in before the first question is opened at the end."
