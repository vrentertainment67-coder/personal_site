Corporate event entertainment works best when it fits the people in the room and the plan for the evening. Start with the event's purpose, then match the music, visuals and schedule to it. I'm [DJ VIC](/), a Bengaluru DJ, and I perform across India and abroad with audio-visual sets that pair curated music with custom visuals.

## Step 1: Set the event goal

Write down what you want guests to do or feel. A company celebration may need a lively dance floor after dinner. A product launch may call for music that leaves space for a welcome and reveal. Be specific about the mood and the moments that matter.

Also note what should not happen. If the event includes formal speeches, music should pause or soften at the right time. This simple brief gives your performer a clear job.

## Step 2: Map the event flow

List the main parts of the night in order: guest arrival, welcome, dinner, speeches, awards or launch moments, then social time. Mark the moments when guests need to listen. That keeps music from competing with the host or a key announcement.

Share the run sheet with your DJ and tell them which timings are fixed. If the schedule shifts, agree who will pass along updates on the day.

I work with corporate organisers on the music flow around speeches and dinner. If you're planning an event in Bengaluru, see my page for a [corporate event DJ in Bangalore](/corporate/).

## Step 3: Choose a format that fits the room

Think about how you want guests to take part. A DJ can shape the music across dinner and dancing. A host can guide formal programme moments. A live act or an interactive performance may suit an event built around a feature slot. These formats do different jobs, so choose based on your schedule and audience, not a trend.

For many company gatherings, the music needs to work for guests with different ages and tastes. My sets blend Bollywood, commercial and regional music, and my signature sound is Bollywood x Disco. Share the audience mix and any music preferences before the set is planned.

Custom visuals can add a visual layer to a DJ performance. If your event has brand content, ask what files are needed, who approves them and when they must be ready.

## Step 4: Write a clear booking brief

Put the core details in one message: date, city, venue, guest count, event type, schedule and the atmosphere you want. Add any key moments, brand needs, music requests or songs to avoid. If something is still being decided, mark it as tentative.

Include the venue contact and a simple room plan if you have one. Note the performance area, setup window and any sound or visual equipment the venue will provide. This helps the performer spot questions before event day.

**Planning tip:** Ask for a written summary of the agreed performance scope, timings and who handles each setup task.

## Step 5: Check fit and booking details

Ask how the performer plans to handle your event's schedule and audience. For a DJ, useful questions include how music choices are discussed, how speeches and dinner fit into the flow, and how special requests are shared. Ask how the performer will get updates if the programme runs late.

Then confirm the details in writing. The agreement should reflect the date, location, performance timing, agreed scope and payment terms. Check the cancellation and rescheduling terms as well, so your team knows what happens if plans change.

I've been performing since 2007 and have played more than 1,400 nights. My signature format brings music and visuals together. That history can help you judge fit, but your event brief still matters most.

## Step 6: Plan the budget and setup

Set a budget range for the full booking, then ask what the proposal includes. The fee can depend on the event type, date, city, duration and sound or lighting requirements. Confirm whether travel or any extra production needs are part of the scope before your team signs off.

Make a separate list of what the venue supplies and what the performer or event team supplies. Confirm the setup window, access to the performance area and who will coordinate with the venue. Never rely on a vague note that the venue has equipment. Ask for the specific setup plan.

My performances bring curated music and custom visuals together, so I confirm the exact audio, lighting and visual requirements for your venue as part of the booking conversation.

> **Key takeaway:** Agree on the full scope and setup responsibilities before you finalise the booking.

## Step 7: Plan for your city and guests

For events in Bengaluru, check the venue's load-in time, performance cut-off and any sound rules. For events in Mumbai, Delhi or another city, confirm travel plans and who will be the on-site contact. I'm based in Bengaluru and available across India and internationally, so include the location when you enquire.

Consider the room as well as the city. An indoor ballroom, outdoor lawn and conference space can each call for a different layout and timing plan. Share any access limits, stage details or visual needs early, then ask the venue to confirm what can be supported.

For a mixed-age group, give the DJ a sense of the crowd and the kinds of music people may respond to. If your team wants Bollywood, retro or commercial tracks, include that in the brief. I also release weekly Bollywood x Disco remixes. You can hear them on my [remixes page](/remixes/).

## Step 8: Confirm the day-of plan

A few days before the event, check that the schedule, venue access and setup plan still match. Make sure the performer has the latest version of the run sheet and knows who can approve changes. Agree how the host, venue contact and performer will communicate during the event.

After the event, ask your team what worked. You can review attendance, feedback and whether key programme moments ran as planned. If you hold the event each year, use those notes to shape the next brief.

## FAQs

### How do I choose entertainment for a corporate event?

Choose a format that supports the event's purpose and schedule. Decide if guests should listen, talk, dance or take part in a feature moment. Then check the performer's fit with your audience and venue. Share the run sheet, music preferences and any key announcements before confirming the booking.

### How much does corporate event entertainment cost?

The cost depends on the event, date, location, duration and setup needs. Ask for a proposal based on your actual brief, then check what it includes. Confirm whether travel, sound or lighting needs and any other production tasks are part of the agreed scope before seeking internal approval.

### What should I ask a DJ before booking?

Ask how the music will be planned, how the DJ will work around speeches and dinner, and how the team can share timing updates on the day. Confirm the performance scope and setup needs in writing. It also helps to ask how you can share must-play songs or tracks you'd prefer to avoid.
