## What to confirm before booking a wedding DJ

A quote is easier to judge when you know what it covers. Ask your DJ to set out the performance time and the sound or lighting equipment included. Then check whether the venue has its own sound rules or supplier requirements.

- **Function plan:** List each event, its start time and the moments that need music cues, such as entries or dance performances.
- **Music brief:** Share must-play tracks and do-not-play requests. Mention language preferences and the kind of energy you want at different points.
- **Production:** Confirm the sound setup for the room and guest count. Ask which lighting or effects are included and who handles setup.
- **Timing:** Agree on set length, venue access for setup and the process for any extra time.
- **Written terms:** Check the event date, venue, agreed services, payment milestones and cancellation terms before signing.
- **Contingencies:** Ask what happens if equipment fails, the schedule changes or the event runs late.

For an outdoor function, confirm the venue's permitted sound hours before you set the dance-floor schedule. For a destination wedding, also agree on travel and setup logistics in writing. These checks can prevent a mismatch between the quote and what the event actually needs.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a wedding DJ cost in Bangalore?

The fee depends on the date, venue, performance length and the sound or lighting setup you need. Ask for a written quote that separates the DJ performance from equipment, travel and any extra hours. DJ VIC's pricing is enquiry-based, so share the event details to request a tailored quote.

### Can one DJ play at the sangeet and reception?

Yes, one DJ can be booked for both functions if the date, schedule and venue logistics allow it. Discuss how the music plan will differ between the events, especially if there are performances or formal moments. If the venues are far apart or the functions overlap, ask how equipment transport and setup will be managed.

### What music should we request for a mixed-age wedding crowd?

Share the languages and genres your guests enjoy, then give the DJ a short list of must-play and do-not-play tracks. You can also describe the mood you want at each stage, such as a relaxed start followed by a lively dance floor. A good brief leaves room for the DJ to read the room and adjust.

### What should a wedding DJ contract include?

A written agreement should identify the date and venue, performance hours, services and equipment covered, payment terms and cancellation conditions. Ask how extra time is handled and what happens if the schedule changes. Make sure any agreed music cues or special moments are recorded in the event plan, rather than left to a last-minute conversation.

### How do I check whether a DJ is right for my wedding?

Ask for a performance clip that resembles your event, then discuss your run sheet and music preferences. Check how the DJ handles transitions around speeches, performances and dinner. You can also read reviews for details about communication and event flow, but confirm that the experience described matches the function you're planning.
